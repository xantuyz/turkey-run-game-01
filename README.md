**Gravy Train — A Turkey's Great Escape.** Thanksgiving morning, the farmer is coming, and one brave turkey is not about to become dinner. Sprint, jump and slide through three stages — the Farmyard, the Barn and the Kitchen — dodging fences, hay bales, falling carving knives and bursts of oven flame while the farmer patrols, spots you, chases you down and winds up a telegraphed lunge. Gobble to startle him, hide in the barn's dark corners, grab corn and gravy boats, and make it out of the kitchen alive.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — Space / Up / click to jump, Down / S to slide, Shift to sprint, G or right-click to gobble, P to pause, M to mute, R to restart.

All art is drawn in code and all sound is synthesized with WebAudio — no downloaded assets, no network requests. The game runs entirely from the static files under `game/`.
